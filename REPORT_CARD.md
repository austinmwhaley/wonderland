## Goal

Input an event stream → train one self-supervised encoder → freeze it → use it as the foundation for supervised, unsupervised, and RL plugins. **B** judges the encoder alone; **B+C** judges the encoder plus six plugin heads (2 per group). Layer-1 decision-sufficiency is explicitly out of scope here and remains open in the audit.
