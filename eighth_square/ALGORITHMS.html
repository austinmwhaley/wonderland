a2c actor-critic on-policy both both Mnih et al. 2016 (A3C paper) paper Synchronous advantage actor-critic over N parallel environments. Uses gymnasium vector envs (default 8); n-step advantage estimates. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot, pendulum, mountaincar_continuous
acer actor-critic off-policy discrete continuous Wang et al. 2017, ICML paper Sample-efficient actor-critic: episode retrace targets, truncated IS with bias correction, efficient-TRPO trust region. Works well on cartpole; weak on acrobot - the Q-based advantage gives no signal while the behavior policy is uniform (constant rewards). cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
acktr actor-critic on-policy discrete continuous Wu et al. 2017, ICML paper A2C with Kronecker-factored natural-gradient updates (K-FAC) instead of Adam. Works on cartpole; weak on acrobot - KFAC conditioning amplifies noise on tiny action spaces. Fisher per Linear layer: nat = G^-1 * grad * A^-1. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
actor_critic actor-critic on-policy both both Sutton & Barto Ch. 13 paper One-step actor-critic: policy and value trained together on TD error. Lower variance than REINFORCE but biased. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot, pendulum, mountaincar_continuous
agent57 value-based off-policy discrete continuous Badia et al. 2020, Nature paper R2D2 with a meta-controller that balances exploration (beta) and long-term credit (gamma) via a UCB bandit over parameterizations. RND-style intrinsic reward (std-normalized) scaled by beta; one value head per discount; round-robin seeding then sliding-window UCB selects the active parameterization. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
alphazero model-based on-policy discrete continuous Silver et al. 2018, Science paper AlphaZero: self-play MCTS with PUCT guided by a learned policy/value network trained on visit-count policies and episode returns. Leaf values blend the network with real model rollouts - a naive value-only backup gives no gradient on dense-reward tasks. Cartpole ~224-244 after 6k steps. cartpole, mountaincar, acrobot
c51 distributional off-policy discrete continuous Bellemare et al. 2017, ICML paper Categorical DQN: learns the full return distribution over 51 atoms. Learns value distributions instead of point estimates; Rainbow ingredient. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
cql value-based off-policy discrete continuous Kumar et al. 2020, NeurIPS paper Conservative Q-Learning: offline Q-learning with a penalty on out-of-distribution actions (log-sum-exp Q minus the Q of the data action). Dataset collected once from an epsilon-greedy DQN behavior policy (eps 0.3 keeps action diversity); polyak target Q; verified on cartpole (107) and acrobot (-75). cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
ddpg actor-critic off-policy continuous continuous Lillicrap et al. 2016, ICLR paper Deep deterministic policy gradient: off-policy actor-critic for continuous actions. Fragile to hyperparameters; TD3 and SAC are its descendants. pendulum, mountaincar_continuous
decision_transformer other off-policy discrete continuous Chen et al. 2021, NeurIPS paper Decision Transformer: causal transformer over (return-to-go, state, action) tokens that predicts actions conditioned on a target return, framing RL as sequence modeling. Trained on behavior-policy episodes; eval conditions on the best dataset return; trajectories tracked internally (reset_episode hook); verified on cartpole (~105). cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
double_dqn value-based off-policy discrete continuous van Hasselt et al. 2016, AAAI paper Decouples action selection (online net) from evaluation (target net) to reduce overestimation. A one-line change to DQN with measurable improvement. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
double_qlearning tabular-value off-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 6.7 paper Uses two Q tables to decouple action selection and evaluation, reducing maximization bias. The tabular ancestor of Double DQN. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
dqn value-based off-policy discrete continuous Mnih et al. 2015, Nature paper Deep Q-network: neural Q with replay buffer and target network. The algorithm that started deep RL. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
dreamer model-based off-policy discrete continuous Hafner et al. 2020, ICLR paper Dreamer: an RSSM world model (deterministic GRU state + stochastic latent) is trained with reconstruction, reward, continuation and free-bits KL; an actor-critic then learns in imagination. The continuation head needs class weighting and the terminal transition in the data, or dreams never end and the actor gets no signal. Cartpole ~30-130. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
dueling_dqn value-based off-policy discrete continuous Wang et al. 2016, ICML paper Separates value and advantage streams in the Q network. Helps in environments where many actions have similar values. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
dyna_q model-based off-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 8 paper Q-learning augmented with a learned model and simulated planning steps. The simplest integration of learning and planning. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
dyna_q_plus model-based off-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 8 paper Dyna-Q with a bonus for states/actions not visited recently. The bonus encourages revisiting stale model entries. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
emphatic_td value-based off-policy discrete tabular Sutton et al. 2016, AIJ paper Emphatic TD: re-weights the TD update with an emphasis M_t = lambdarhoM_{t-1} + 1. Emphasis grows when the target policy visits a state more than behavior does. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
epsilon_greedy_bandit bandit none discrete bandit Sutton & Barto Ch. 2 paper Sample-average action values with epsilon-greedy action selection. The default baseline every other bandit algorithm is compared against. bandit
expected_sarsa tabular-value on-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 6 paper SARSA variant that bootstraps with the expected value over actions. Reduces variance vs SARSA at the cost of one expectation per update. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
gradient_bandit bandit none discrete bandit Sutton & Barto Ch. 2 paper Softmax action preferences learned via stochastic gradient ascent with baseline. Uses average reward as baseline; sensitive to step size. bandit
gtd value-based off-policy discrete tabular Sutton et al. 2009 paper GTD2 gradient TD: off-policy linear prediction with a second weight vector for the covariance correction. Behavior is uniform random; target is uniform over the first two actions, so IS ratios are 2/0. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
her value-based off-policy discrete continuous Andrychowicz et al. 2017, NeurIPS paper Hindsight Experience Replay: failed episodes are relabeled with goals the agent actually reached, converting sparse rewards into dense ones for goal-conditioned value learning. DQN core with goal as part of the observation; future-state relabeling (k=4 copies per transition). Learns point_reach (~40-50 step horizon) where plain sparse DQN stays at ~0 reward. point_reach
icm value-based off-policy discrete both Pathak et al. 2017, ICML paper Intrinsic Curiosity Module: DQN plus a learned forward model whose prediction error is an intrinsic reward. Inverse model ties the features to actionability; intrinsic reward is std-normalized. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot, cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
impala actor-critic off-policy discrete continuous Espeholt et al. 2018, ICML paper V-trace: parallel actor-learners with clipped importance ratios for the value target (rho) and the trace (c). Single-learner port: ratios are ~1, so the policy gradient is the on-policy one; validated on cartpole and acrobot. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
iql value-based off-policy discrete continuous Kostrikov et al. 2022, NeurIPS paper Implicit Q-Learning: the value function is fit with expectile regression so OOD actions never inflate the target; the policy is advantage-weighted behavioral cloning. Expectile tau=0.7 with a polyak target-V; AWR weights use mean-abs-scaled advantages; verified on cartpole (500); acrobot is unstable at smoke scale (needs more diverse data than the 40k budget). cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
maddpg multi-agent off-policy discrete continuous Lowe et al. 2017, NeurIPS paper MADDPG: each agent runs a policy while a centralized critic sees the joint observation and joint actions (CTDE), with target networks and a joint TD target. Discrete-action port: double-Q joint targets (argmax from the online critic, value from the target) and per-agent softmax actors with greedy-Q targets. On cooperative_cartpole (anti-correlated optimal) the batch-averaged policy gradient averages out symmetric state advantages and the actors collapse to correlated same-direction play (ret ~8-12 vs random 23, oscillate 43, QMIX 93). cooperative_cartpole
mappo multi-agent on-policy discrete continuous Yu et al. 2022, ICLR paper MAPPO: independent PPO policies per agent trained on the same GAE advantages from a centralized critic over the joint state (CTDE). Shared advantages make both policies drift symmetrically, so anti-correlated coordination is never discovered; ret ~9-12 on cooperative_cartpole even at 60k steps. cooperative_cartpole
mc_control_offpolicy tabular-value off-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 5 paper Off-policy MC control via importance sampling toward a greedy target. Only episodes consistent with the greedy target contribute updates. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
mc_control_onpolicy tabular-value on-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 5 paper On-policy first-visit MC control with epsilon-soft policies. Waits for episode completion before updating; unbiased but noisy. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
mc_prediction tabular-value none discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 5 paper First- or every-visit Monte Carlo prediction of a fixed (random) policy. Evaluates the random policy; use the control variants to learn behavior. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
mcts model-based on-policy discrete continuous Coulom 2006 / S&B Ch. 8 paper Monte Carlo Tree Search: UCB1 selection, expansion, random rollouts, mean-return backup. Plans in the real environment via state cloning. Pure planning, no learning; cartpole ~160-250 returns with 100 iterations. The real env (state cloning) is the model. cartpole, mountaincar, acrobot
muzero model-based on-policy discrete continuous Schrittwieser et al. 2019, Nature paper MuZero: learns a latent model (representation, dynamics with reward and continuation, policy/value prediction) and plans in it with MCTS. Latent-consistency loss and a continuation head are required for dense-reward tasks; a one-ply real lookahead breaks the random-policy bootstrap. Cartpole ~100-500. cartpole, mountaincar, acrobot
naive_qlearning value-based off-policy discrete continuous Sutton & Barto Ch. 11 (the naive method) paper Online semi-gradient Q-learning: naive off-policy bootstrapping with a neural Q, no replay or target network. Off-policy + bootstrapping + function approximation: the classic recipe for divergence. Compare with dqn to see what replay and target networks fix. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
nstep_expected_sarsa tabular-value on-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 7 paper n-step SARSA bootstrapping with the expected next-state value. Combines n-step returns with expected-value bootstrapping. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
nstep_off_policy tabular-value off-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 7 paper n-step off-policy SARSA with per-decision importance sampling. Updates only when the behavior actions match the greedy target. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
nstep_sarsa tabular-value on-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 7 paper n-step SARSA: bootstraps after n steps instead of one. n=1 is SARSA; large n approaches MC control. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
optimistic_bandit bandit none discrete bandit Sutton & Barto Ch. 2 paper Greedy action selection with optimistically initialized Q values. Simple and effective: initial optimism alone drives exploration. bandit
policy_evaluation planning none discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 4 paper Iterative DP policy evaluation on the environment model (.P). Model-based: requires an env with explicit transition probabilities. frozenlake, cliffwalking
policy_iteration planning none discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 4 paper Alternates policy evaluation and greedy improvement until stable. Converges to the optimal policy for finite MDPs. frozenlake, cliffwalking
ppo policy-gradient on-policy both both Schulman et al. 2017, arXiv:1707.06347 paper Proximal policy optimization with clipped surrogate objective and GAE. The workhorse of modern RL; robust across seeds and tasks. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot, pendulum, mountaincar_continuous
prioritized_dqn value-based off-policy discrete continuous Schaul et al. 2016, ICLR paper Samples transitions by TD-error magnitude with importance correction (also uses double Q). A sum-tree buffer drives replay toward surprising transitions. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
prioritized_sweeping model-based off-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 8 paper Model-based planning that replays the transitions with the largest TD errors. Backs up predecessors of high-error transitions via a priority queue. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
q_lambda tabular-value off-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 12 paper Watkins Q(lambda): off-policy traces that cut at exploratory actions. Traces are reset when the behavior action is non-greedy. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
qlearning tabular-value off-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 6 paper Off-policy TD(0): learns the optimal Q independent of behavior policy. The most famous TD control algorithm; optimistic, so risk-prone in cliffs. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
qmix multi-agent off-policy discrete continuous Rashid et al. 2018, ICML paper QMIX: per-agent Q-networks whose values are mixed by a state-dependent nonnegative (IGM-preserving) hypernetwork into a joint Q trained with joint TD. Learns the anti-correlated cooperative policy that the independent-policy CTDE methods cannot: ret ~91-93 on cooperative_cartpole. Per-sample Q fitting preserves the state-conditional action ordering that batch-averaged actor losses destroy. cooperative_cartpole
qr_dqn distributional off-policy discrete continuous Dabney et al. 2018, ICML paper Quantile regression DQN: learns quantiles of the return distribution. A flexible successor to C51 with quantile Huber loss. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
r2d2 value-based off-policy discrete continuous Kapturowski et al. 2019, ICLR paper Recurrent Replay DQN: LSTM Q-network trained on prioritized sequences with burn-in, n-step returns, and value rescaling. Single-learner port: sequences replayed a bounded number of times; targets computed in the rescaled space h(x). cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
rainbow value-based off-policy discrete continuous Hessel et al. 2018, AAAI paper Combines double Q, dueling, prioritized replay, noisy nets, n-step returns, and C51. Each ingredient contributes; run ablations by turning flags off. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
reinforce policy-gradient on-policy both both Sutton & Barto Ch. 13 paper REINFORCE: unbiased policy gradient using full-episode returns. High variance; needs small learning rates. baseline=True removes the baseline. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot, pendulum, mountaincar_continuous
reinforce_baseline policy-gradient on-policy both both Sutton & Barto Ch. 13 paper REINFORCE with a learned value-function baseline to reduce variance. Same update rule as reinforce but subtracts a critic estimate. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot, pendulum, mountaincar_continuous
rnd value-based off-policy discrete both Burda et al. 2018, ICLR paper Random Network Distillation: DQN plus an intrinsic reward from a frozen random network the predictor cannot match on novel states. Simpler than ICM: no inverse model, bonus uses only the next state. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot, cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
sac actor-critic off-policy continuous continuous Haarnoja et al. 2018, ICML paper Soft actor-critic: maximum-entropy RL with stochastic policy and automatic temperature. The default choice for continuous control. pendulum, mountaincar_continuous
sarsa tabular-value on-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 6 paper On-policy TD(0) control: learns Q for the policy being followed. Safer than Q-learning in risky environments (e.g., CliffWalking). cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
sarsa_lambda tabular-value on-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 12 paper SARSA(lambda) with accumulating or replacing eligibility traces. One update per step but propagates credit across many steps. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
semi_gradient_sarsa value-based on-policy discrete continuous Sutton & Barto Ch. 10 paper Online semi-gradient TD(0) control with a neural Q approximator. No replay or target network: the honest, unstable ancestor of DQN. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
td0_prediction tabular-value none discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 6 paper TD(0) prediction: bootstrap value estimates from the next state. Evaluates the random policy; the control workhorses are SARSA/Q-learning. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
td3 actor-critic off-policy continuous continuous Fujimoto et al. 2018, ICML paper Twin delayed DDPG: clipped double-Q, delayed policy updates, target smoothing. Addresses DDPG's value overestimation with three targeted fixes. pendulum, mountaincar_continuous
td_lambda_prediction tabular-value none discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 12 paper TD(lambda) prediction with eligibility traces. lambda=0 is TD(0), lambda=1 approaches MC. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
tde value-based on-policy discrete tabular van Seijen et al. 2014 paper True Online TD(lambda): dutch eligibility trace with the correction term that makes the per-step update exact. O(d) per step with the dutch trace; on-policy here so the trace stays bounded. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
thompson_sampling bandit none discrete bandit Thompson 1933 paper Sample actions from a posterior over arm means (Gaussian posterior). Modern addition; the gold standard for bandit exploration. bandit
tree_backup tabular-value off-policy discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 7 paper n-step off-policy TD control without importance sampling. Bootstraps with an expectation over the target policy; no IS ratios. cliffwalking, frozenlake, blackjack, bandit
trpo policy-gradient on-policy both both Schulman et al. 2015, arXiv:1502.05477 paper Trust region policy optimization: KL-constrained updates via conjugate gradient. The theoretical predecessor of PPO; slower but provably monotone. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot, pendulum, mountaincar_continuous
ucb_bandit bandit none discrete bandit Sutton & Barto Ch. 2 paper Upper-confidence-bound action selection: Q + c * sqrt(ln t / N). Selects actions by uncertainty bonus; no explicit epsilon needed. bandit
value_iteration planning none discrete tabular Sutton & Barto Ch. 4 paper In-place Bellman optimality backups until convergence. Equivalent to policy iteration with truncated evaluation; faster in practice. frozenlake, cliffwalking
world_models model-based off-policy discrete continuous Ha & Schmidhuber 2018 paper World Models: a VAE compresses observations, an MDN-RNN learns the latent dynamics, and a linear controller is trained by CEM inside dreams. Works purely from random data: cartpole ~40-130 with the CEM controller on imagined rollouts. cartpole, lunar, mountaincar, acrobot
